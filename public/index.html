How to fly

  1. 1

    Tune. Pick a band, then turn the knob, swipe the screen, or tap a ship on ◎ RADAR.

  2. 2

    Push. X opens the room. Press its Start listening.

  3. 3

    Listen. Tap I HEAR IT. To talk, request the mic in the X app (MIC hands the room to your phone).

Hearing nothing?
  1. Find the X window beside the radio (or a new tab) and press Start listening. Logged out, it says Start listening anonymously.
  2. Check that the X tab isn't muted, and your volume.
  3. Rooms go quiet between speakers. Give it 20 seconds.
  4. Still nothing? The room may have ended. Flip to the next one.

Why two presses? Browsers only let a site make sound after you press something on it. The radio finds the room; X opens the hatch.

Docking with a friend

Like Rocky and Grace at the clear wall: two radios link through a tunnel, and each of you keeps your own dial and your own X. DOCK ⟷ gives you a code like ERID-42; send its link to one friend.

Tones: ✊ hello · ✦ this one's good · ↑ come to my room · ↓ moving on. The real talking happens inside the Space.

What costs money
  • Up to 10¢: a band nobody opened in the last hour. After that it's shared, free.
  • About 10–15¢: WHO'S HERE on a busy room.
  • Free: flipping, radar, PUSH, OPEN IN X, beam, docking, presets.

Tap the ⛽ gauge for today's log. If fuel runs out, the last rooms found keep playing.
