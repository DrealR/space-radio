Owner's manual

A walkie-talkie radio for live X Spaces. Every channel is a room of real people talking right now. Flip through them the way you'd flip through a feed.

  1. BAND picks what it's about. Rooms on the glass are live, and taller ones are busier.
  2. TUNE: turn the knob, swipe the screen, or press ← →.
  3. PUSH TO JOIN opens the room in X (the app, on a phone). Listen, or request the mic.
  4. SCAN (on a computer) hops to a new room every few minutes while you work.

Program a preset

Found a live room on your phone or in a post? Paste its link and it gets a preset button. Presets live in this browser only.
